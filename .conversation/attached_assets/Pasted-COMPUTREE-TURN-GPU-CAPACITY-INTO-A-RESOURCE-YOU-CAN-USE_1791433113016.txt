COMPUTREE

TURN GPU CAPACITY INTO A RESOURCE YOU CAN USE.

Computree is an access layer for NVIDIA GPU compute.

It acquires GPU capacity from the infrastructure market and makes that capacity available through a programmable API.

Eligible $CTREE holders receive a daily allocation of compute, refreshed every 24 hours, and can use it across supported applications and workloads.

GPU COMPUTE IS BECOMING INFRASTRUCTURE.

The demand for accelerated computing is growing faster than most users can provision it.

GPU hardware is expensive. Cloud infrastructure is fragmented. Capacity changes constantly.

Computree abstracts that complexity into a single compute access layer.

Instead of owning the machine, you access the capacity.

Instead of managing the infrastructure, you use the API.

Instead of paying for an individual GPU fleet, Computree acquires capacity from the infrastructure market and distributes access through the network.

$CTREE DETERMINES YOUR COMPUTE ALLOCATION.

$CTREE is the access mechanism for the Computree network.

Eligible holders receive compute allocation based on their qualifying token balance.

The more eligible $CTREE associated with your account, the greater your available daily allocation.

Your allocation can be consumed across supported workloads and applications.

It is not ownership of a specific machine.

It is access to compute capacity acquired by the network.

⸻

YOUR ALLOCATION REFRESHES EVERY 24 HOURS.

Computree is designed around a recurring compute allocation rather than a traditional cloud subscription.

At the beginning of each cycle, your eligible $CTREE balance determines your available allocation.

Use your compute throughout the day.

Your remaining allocation expires at the end of the cycle.

The next cycle begins with a fresh allocation.

THE NETWORK ACQUIRES THE COMPUTE.

Computree separates compute acquisition from compute consumption.

The network can acquire GPU capacity from supported infrastructure providers and compute networks.

The initial infrastructure layer can use decentralized compute infrastructure such as Golem, while additional GPU providers can be integrated over time.

This allows Computree to expand its available capacity without requiring users to establish and manage individual infrastructure accounts.

NVIDIA GPU INFRASTRUCTURE.

NVIDIA GPUs form the primary hardware layer for supported accelerated workloads.

Computree does not require users to own or operate those machines.

Instead, the network can acquire capacity from infrastructure providers that operate NVIDIA GPU environments.

The provider layer can evolve over time.

The Computree API remains consistent.

⸻

ONE ACCESS LAYER. MULTIPLE INFRASTRUCTURE SOURCES.

Computree is designed so the public API does not depend on a single infrastructure provider.

A provider can supply compute capacity without becoming the product itself.

This creates a modular infrastructure layer where additional GPU providers can be added according to availability, pricing, geography, hardware and workload requirements.

The user sees one system.

Computree manages the underlying infrastructure.
